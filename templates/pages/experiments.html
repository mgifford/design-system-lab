Experiments

An experiment is a working proposal with a category, baseline, implementation guidance, evidence status, and a human-review record. Absence of a stable USWDS component is described as a gap in capability, not as an accessibility failure.

How an experiment moves

A prototype is not an approval. Human review is a separate, recorded decision. An experiment may end as documentation or a rejected proposal; not every result should become an upstream pull request. Read governance and the lifecycle.
