What to compare

  • Header and primary navigation: weight, hierarchy, and search placement.
  • Banner prominence and the amount of vertical space it takes.
  • Hero treatment, heading typography, and the primary call to action.
  • Card shape, borders, and heading style.
  • Accordion controls and panel boundaries.
  • Alert emphasis and footer density.

What is not being claimed

The right pane is not OGDS Elements and does not predict its exact appearance. It is a controlled visual alternative built from the same content so a department can evaluate the comparison experience before the licensed OGDS integration exists.

Automated screenshots, axe results, and Guidepup transcripts support inspection. They are not a WCAG conformance statement or a design verdict.

How this becomes the OGDS comparison

The left pane stays pinned to USWDS 3.14.0. The right pane is generated from the same content model and can be swapped to a licensed OGDS hybrid renderer without changing the comparison shell. Components with no OGDS equivalent remain USWDS and are labelled as unchanged.
