How candidates become experiments

A contributor links the original issue and its important follow-ups, writes a problem statement and acceptance criteria, implements an isolated proposal, runs repeatable tests, and prepares Markdown evidence. A maintainer decides whether to recommend, revise, reject, or contribute the change upstream.

Status meanings are deliberate: Completed work is a regression reference, not a missing feature; Consolidated work is tracked elsewhere; Closed, not planned is not evidence that maintainers rejected the need. Read governance requirements.
