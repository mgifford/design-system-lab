Evidence and coverage

Every published lab route has a deterministic browser test and a Guidepup virtual transcript when collection runs. Missing output is shown as a coverage gap. Automated observations are not human review and do not establish WCAG conformance.

How to interpret this evidence

Playwright tests check defined assertions. A pass is not a WCAG conformance claim. Guidepup uses a virtual screen reader over a DOM snapshot; it does not reproduce actual VoiceOver, NVDA, or JAWS operation. Axe records computable rule violations but cannot judge whether content, names, or interactions are meaningful. Human review is a separate status, never inferred from automated output.

Read the evidence method, the test plan, and the accessibility commitment.

What is not tested

  • Real NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver, or TalkBack sessions.
  • Voice Control or speech-input operation of the radio and form fixtures.
  • Windows High Contrast Mode on a real Windows device; forced-colors emulation is a browser approximation.
  • Every browser/OS/zoom/text-spacing combination.
  • Adoption, maintenance cost, or upstream compatibility beyond the recorded release.
