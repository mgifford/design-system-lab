How to read a result

Baseline panes load the pinned USWDS 3.14.0 distribution. The experimental pane adds a separate stylesheet and, where relevant, small markup changes. Guidepup transcripts are virtual screen-reader output over a DOM snapshot; they are not NVDA, JAWS, or VoiceOver sessions. Automated results are observations, not approval.
