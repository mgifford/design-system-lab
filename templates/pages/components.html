What “baseline” means here

The baseline is the pinned npm distribution plus authored fixture markup and a small fixture stylesheet. It is not a byte-for-byte copy of every official example. The experimental pane is a separate MIT-licensed layer over the same fixture. See methodology for provenance and limitations.
