How candidates become experiments

A contributor links the original issue and its important follow-ups, writes a problem statement and acceptance criteria, implements an isolated proposal, runs repeatable tests, and prepares a Markdown evidence report. Maintainers decide whether to recommend, revise, reject, or contribute the change upstream.

Proposals are not silently counted as implemented. Read governance requirements.

Independent work, with full upstream links and no implied endorsement.
